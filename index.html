How it works

  1. Drop your files. Mix any of the supported formats. Large files are fine.
  2. They are converted on your device. Nothing is sent to a server. Headings, lists, tables and slide titles are kept; repeated headers, footers and page numbers are removed.
  3. Download the Markdown. Save one file at a time, copy it, or download everything as a .zip.

What you get

PDF
Headings from font size and bold text, paragraphs and bullet lists, headers and footers removed, spaced-out titles like “C H A P T E R” rejoined, OCR for scanned pages.
Word .docx .doc
Headings, lists and tables. Images are skipped.
PowerPoint .pptx .ppt
Slide titles, bullet levels, tables, charts as data tables, SmartArt text, image alt text and speaker notes.
Excel .xlsx .xls
Each sheet as a table, with empty rows and columns removed.

Your files never leave your browser.

Open source on GitHub. Built with pdf.js, Mammoth, Turndown, Tesseract.js and office_oxide.
